FACEIT is a competitive gaming platform. You still play a real title — most often Counter-Strike 2 — inside FACEIT’s client, rankings, and events. It is not a shooter you buy instead of CS2, Valorant, Overwatch 2, or Rainbow Six Siege.

That distinction is why this site is built as a [FACEIT provider overview](/) with separate game routes, not one article that treats “FACEIT” as the product.

## FACEIT is the queue, not the game

Players search “what is FACEIT” when they see the name next to a rank, a client, or a third-party ladder. The short answer: FACEIT hosts matches for games you already own or install. The long answer is organizational. FACEIT adds its own matchmaking rules, anti-cheat environment, and ranking on top of the title.

You do not install “FACEIT the game.” You launch CS2 (or another supported title) into that ecosystem.

## Why matchmaking language gets confusing

Regular matchmaking is whatever the game publisher already ships. FACEIT matchmaking is a parallel competitive path. Same maps and weapons in CS2; different client, ladder, and platform rules.

People then search for FACEIT cheats or a FACEIT private cheat as if FACEIT itself were the software. On this site those phrases point at tools for a named game in that ecosystem. The [site-wide FAQ](/faq/) keeps the platform questions; each game page keeps the listing.
